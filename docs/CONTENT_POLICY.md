# Editorial and Platform-Safety Policy

## Brand pillars
1. Full-stack and backend engineering
2. React, Node.js, JavaScript, TypeScript, MongoDB
3. AI tools and practical AI engineering
4. Developer productivity and learning
5. Building projects and lessons learned
6. Startups and product engineering

## Voice
- Clear, useful, specific, human, technically accurate.
- Prefer concrete examples over generic motivation.
- Avoid fake controversy, engagement bait, excessive emojis, and hashtag stuffing.
- Do not pretend the account owner tested a tool or built a feature unless they supplied that fact.
- Do not invent project metrics, credentials, employers, awards, user counts, or personal stories.

## Research rules
- Cite source URLs internally for current factual claims.
- Prefer primary sources and official documentation.
- Record publication date and retrieval date.
- Separate confirmed facts from interpretation.
- If a claim cannot be verified, remove it or label it as opinion.
- Do not copy source wording; summarize and add original analysis.

## Content checks
- Current platform character limits must be validated against current X documentation.
- Check duplicate/near-duplicate content.
- Check misleading claims and unsupported statistics.
- Check privacy and confidential information.
- Check for personal attacks, harassment, hate, or dangerous instructions.
- Do not generate fake testimonials or fabricated screenshots/results.

## Engagement
- Research and suggest relevant conversations.
- Draft contextual replies for owner review.
- No automated likes, follows, unfollows, mass replies, unsolicited replies, or repetitive spam.
- Never impersonate another person or simulate organic engagement.

## Publishing
- Human approval is mandatory in MVP.
- Approval is invalidated if text or target channel changes.
- Respect platform and Buffer policies, API limits, and account restrictions.
